Chapter 11 · Automated Planning

Chapter 3 searched a hand-coded state space. Classical planning describes the same problem in a factored language (PDDL): states are sets of fluents, and actions have preconditions and effects. That structure makes domain-independent heuristics, backward search and SAT encodings possible.
