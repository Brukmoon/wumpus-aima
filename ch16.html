Chapter 16 · Making Simple Decisions

Chapter 12 told the agent how likely each square is to kill it. Decision theory tells it what to do: choose the action with maximum expected utility. The utility function also encodes an attitude to risk. And before acting, it asks what information would be worth buying.

Outcomes are in game points. Dying costs 1000. Going home now is worth 0 (no gold, nothing lost). Surviving a step is worth V, a rough stand-in for “the treasure hunt can continue”.
