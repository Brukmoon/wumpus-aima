Chapter 4 · Search in Complex Environments

Chapter 3 assumed a known, deterministic, fully observable cave. Here we drop those assumptions one at a time. The cave is optimized instead of solved (local search), the floor is slippery (nondeterminism), the agent doesn't know where it is (belief states), and the map is unknown (online search).
