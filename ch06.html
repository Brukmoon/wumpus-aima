Chapter 6 · Adversarial Search and Games

The wumpus wakes up. In this wumpus hunt the map is known and the two sides take turns. The agent (MAX) moves or shoots its one arrow, and wins by bringing the gold back to [1,1]. The wumpus (MIN) moves or stays, and wins by catching the agent. Utilities: win = 1000 − moves − 10 if the arrow was used, loss = −1000, draw after 40 moves = 0.

Evaluation function (§6.3)

EVAL(s) = (has gold ? 400 − 30·dist(agent, [1,1]) : 200 − 30·dist(agent, gold)) − (wumpus adjacent ? 300 : wumpus at distance 2 ? 80 : 0) + (wumpus dead ? 150 : 0) + (arrow ? 20 : 0) − moves

A cutoff search trusts EVAL at the frontier. Look for moves the agent makes because EVAL misjudges a position. That's the horizon effect.
