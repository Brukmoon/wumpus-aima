Chapter 12 · Quantifying Uncertainty

Logic can say only “safe”, “deadly” or “don't know”. When no square is provably safe, the Chapter 7 agent had to guess. Probability tells it how likely each square is to hold a pit or the wumpus. This is the book's own Wumpus example (§12.7), computed exactly for any world and any set of observations.
