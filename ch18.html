Chapter 18 · Multiagent Decision Making

When the outcome depends on what another agent does, “maximize expected utility” needs a model of that agent. Game theory's answer is the Nash equilibrium: a strategy profile in which no player gains by deviating alone. The games below are small Wumpus stories. Edit the payoffs and watch the equilibria move.

Analysis

Fictitious play each player best-responds to the other's observed action frequencies

In two-player zero-sum games the empirical frequencies converge to an equilibrium. In other games they may cycle or settle on one pure equilibrium.
